Playlists

How switching works

Strong 8K and Magnum use separate URL pools so credentials never cross. Strong 8K VPN DNS is a separate lane — auto failover never picks those hosts (a player off VPN cannot use them). Switch stays on the current lane. Choose URL can pick a VPN host when the player is on a VPN. Magnum playlists auto-failover via EPGenius like Strong 8K, but only onto Magnum-tagged URLs. After a Magnum swap, Watch DNS on the VPS (player.yaml) follows the new host and /watch refreshes the live list.

How to read this

One square per standby URL, grouped by lane, in the same place every refresh. Green is up, amber with ! has gone down 3+ times in 24 hours, red with ✕ is down now. Hover or focus a square for details; click it to jump to that host's card below.

Reliability

How grades work

Each standby gets a grade from the share of the last 30 days on which it had at least one outage, as seen from the VPS — scaled to 30 days: A 0–2 days, B 3–6, C 7–12, D 13–20, F 21 or more. Counting days rather than outages means one bad spike only costs a host one day, and days when 75% or more of the pool failed at once are treated as VPS-side incidents and not counted against anyone (faint bars in the strips). Best picks are the most reliable healthy hosts graded C or better in each lane — good targets for Choose URL, which now lists hosts best grade first. Avoid lists hosts graded D or F. Click a host to jump to its card.
